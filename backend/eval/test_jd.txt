Associate Consultant – Brightwater Advisory (Boston)

About us
Brightwater Advisory is a strategy and operations consultancy working with energy, manufacturing and public-sector clients, including education non-profits.

The role
You will join client teams to solve operational and strategic problems: supplier and cost reviews, market-entry studies and performance improvement programmes.

What you will do
- Structure ambiguous problems and build analyses in Excel, SQL or Python
- Interview client stakeholders and turn findings into clear recommendations
- Present to client leadership and persuade them with data
- Work in small teams under tight timelines

What we look for
- 2 to 5 years of experience in operations, supply chain, analytics or consulting
- Strong problem-solving and communication skills
- Evidence of leadership and influencing without authority
- Interest in energy or education sectors is a plus
